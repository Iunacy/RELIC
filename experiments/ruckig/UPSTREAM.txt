Compatibility: official CMake requires C++20; no external dependencies for
offline Community state-to-state trajectories. No Eigen/Python/nanobind needed.
Community restrictions: local intermediate waypoints, positional constraints,
tracking interface and other Pro facilities are not used. Cloud disabled.
Synchronization::Time and DurationDiscretization::Discrete are explicitly set.
Joint positions are independently checked; Community max_position/min_position
are not relied on. Upstream numerical range: limits below 1e9, duration <=7000s;
upstream endpoint guarantees use absolute tolerances 1e-8 p/v and 1e-10 a.
